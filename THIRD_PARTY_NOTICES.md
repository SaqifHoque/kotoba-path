## nihongo dataset collection

The supplied `nihongo` archive combines sources under several licenses. Its original project material is CC BY-SA 4.0. Included component mappings are attributed by that project to Kanji Bakuhatsu (GPL-3.0), word translations to JMdict/EDRDG (CC BY-SA 3.0), component names to Kanji Alive (CC BY 4.0), and Wordlex frequency data to Gimenes & New (2015). Source and detailed attribution are preserved in `datasets/nihongo/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

JLPT bands shown by this application are clearly marked estimates derived from the educational grade field because neither supplied archive provides an official current JLPT mapping. Frequency rank is derived from first appearance in the supplied Wordlex ordering.
