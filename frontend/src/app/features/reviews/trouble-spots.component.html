How this list is chosen

Each skill needs at least 3 saved scheduled misses and under 80% correct across its saved scheduled reviews. Burned skills are excluded. Highest miss counts come first, then lower accuracy. Displayed percentages are rounded; the counts determine inclusion.

These are lifetime counters from your saved typed-course progress, including imported progress. They are not recent history or a prediction of retention. Reading-path ratings and extra practice are excluded.

Correct scheduled reviews can improve these counters and remove a skill from this list. Extra practice leaves counters, review dates, stages, history and quest credit unchanged.
